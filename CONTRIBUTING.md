## Where to edit

In this public repository:

- published content goes under `published/`

Please do not add working notes, drafting fragments, or editorial residue to published files.

Examples of text that should not remain in `published/`:

- translation notes
- editorial reminders
- AI response residue
- "to be linked later" placeholders unless intentionally kept
